Incident report: a phishing campaign impersonating a shipping notification
was observed dropping a payload from http://malicious-drop.example.com/invoice.exe
hosted on 203.0.113.44. The same actor's infrastructure previously resolved
to 198.51.100.7 (c2-panel.example.net) and 192.0.2.19.

The dropped file (SHA256: 3b0c44298fc1c149afbf4c8996fb92427ae41e4649b934ca495991b7852b855)
communicates with a command-and-control endpoint at http://c2-relay.example.org:8443/beacon
and has been associated with exploitation attempts against CVE-2021-44228 (Log4Shell)
and CVE-2023-23397 in prior campaigns.

A secondary sample (MD5: 44d88612fea8a8f36de82e1278abb02c) was staged at
http://secondary-stage.example.com/update.bin, resolving through 203.0.113.101.
Related phishing infrastructure used the domain login-verify.example.net
(192.0.2.88) to harvest credentials before redirecting victims to
http://payload-host.example.org/final.exe.
